Antibody & peptide design

One of the hardest choices in antibody design comes before the experiment. Which sequences are worth synthesizing? We developed IASO to generate single-chain antibody candidates from an antigen’s amino acid sequence alone and rank them by predicted binding. For peptides, we evaluate binding and four safety measures on each candidate to help decide which molecules merit experimental follow-up.
