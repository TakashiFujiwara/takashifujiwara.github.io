Antibody & peptide design

Can we design an antibody for a target protein from its amino acid sequence alone? We developed IASO to generate single-chain antibody candidates and use predicted binding to choose which ones to test in the lab. For peptides, we developed Pepti-drift to generate, in a single step, sequences predicted to bind the target and have low toxicity.
