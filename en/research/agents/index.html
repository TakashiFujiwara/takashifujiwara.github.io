AI agents for drug discovery

Finding a promising target leaves open a second question. How would a molecule designed for it behave in the body? I co-developed OrchestRA so that researchers can choose a target, have a chemistry agent design candidates, and ask a pharmacology agent to predict their absorption and distribution. The chemistry agent then uses that feedback to revise the structures.

Democratizing Drug Discovery with an Orchestrated, Knowledge-Driven Multi-Agent Team for User-Guided Therapeutic Design

Takahide Suzuki (co-1st), Kazuki Nakanishi (co-1st), Takashi Fujiwara (co-1st), Hideyuki Shimizu

arXiv, 2025PreprintPDF — Democratizing Drug Discovery with an Orchestrated, Knowledge-Driven Multi-Agent Team for User-Guided Therapeutic Design

We developed OrchestRA, a multi-agent AI system that brings together steps usually performed separately in drug discovery. It identifies potential drug targets, designs compounds, and predicts their toxicity and pharmacokinetics. Our goal is to make this workflow more widely accessible.
