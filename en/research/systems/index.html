Research

Genomics of disease & regeneration

Parkinson’s disease can make movement hard to initiate, while Huntington’s disease can produce involuntary movements. We used that contrast to look for regulators in patient gene-expression data and tested a candidate in human neurons. In a separate study, I compare species to ask which DNA patterns associated with axolotl limb regeneration are also present in humans and mice.

Axolotl regeneration reveals a dormant cis-regulatory grammar conserved across vertebrate genomes

Takashi Fujiwara, Kazuki Nakanishi, Takahide Suzuki, Hideyuki Shimizu

We found combinations of short DNA motifs associated with axolotl limb regeneration near the corresponding genes in humans and mice. AlphaGenome predicted low transcriptional activity at the strongest mammalian candidate sites.
